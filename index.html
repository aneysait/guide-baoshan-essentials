Arrival Essentials

The two
priorities

Upon your arrival in Shanghai, even before classes start or moving into the dorm, two administrative and practical steps determine your entire stay: getting a Chinese phone number and opening a bank account.

The mandatory order of steps
  • 1. SIM card first (Passport only) : Banks strictly require an active Chinese phone number for SMS verification. Get your SIM card on your first or second day.
  • 2. Bank account next (SIM + Passport + Residence Registration) : With your Chinese number and the residence registration (临时住宿登记单) that you must request at your hotel reception (or register yourself online if you are in an apartment), you can open your account.

Phone
Chapter I

Get a
SIM card

Your phone number is your digital identity in China. It is essential for opening a bank account, ordering on Taobao, or registering for bike-sharing apps.

The three options near campus

China Unicom — Jufengyuan Road 中国联通 聚丰园路营业厅recommended→ go there
155 Jufengyuan Rd · 10–15 min walk from campus
The closest official store to campus, used to SHU students. Unicom is the simplest operator for foreigners: best compatibility with European/foreign phones.
China Telecom — Jufengyuan Road 中国电信 聚丰园路营业厅→ go there
329 Jufengyuan Rd · 15–20 min walk from campus · ~8h30–18h00
The cheapest of the three operators. This branch is very convenient as it is located on the same street as China Unicom, just a few minutes walk away. Ideal for comparing.
China Mobile — Jufengyuan Road 中国移动 聚丰园路营业厅→ go there
189 Jufengyuan Rd · 10–15 min walk from campus
Small official agency (Dachang M-Zone). Very convenient as it is located on the same street as the Unicom branch.
Budget for a semester : 19 to 59 RMB/month. Ask for prepaid with no contract : 预付费，无合约 — and refuse the 12–24 month contracts they will automatically offer you.
  • Keep the number active all semester : a prepaid number with zero balance is suspended then recycled (~90 days) — and with it goes your access to WeChat Pay and your bank. Do small regular top-ups via Alipay.
  • When leaving : cancel in-store (销号) before leaving. Remember to change your phone number on WeChat and Alipay to your home country number.
UTSEUS Tip : come with a Chinese-speaking friend or a UTSEUS buddy — passport registration takes time and the staff in these neighborhood shops rarely speak English.

Money
Chapter II

Open a
bank account

General information (currency, cards, Alipay/WeChat with foreign cards) is in the AFEC guide. Here: branches around campus, and the reality of a one-semester X2 visa — banks decide case by case, so aim for the SHU partner branch as soon as you have the SIM and hotel registration. All ATMs of these 4 major banks have an English interface and accept foreign cards.

The four major banks near campus

Bank of China — Qilianshan Road 中国银行祁连山路支行SHU partner→ go there
165 Jufengyuan Rd · less than 5 min by bike from the west gate
The branch cited in the official SHU international student handbook: they process foreign students all day and the staff is used to passports. By far your best chances.
Game-changing tip : pre-book the opening via the "BOC Appointment for Account Opening" WeChat Mini Program (Foreigner profile, passport photo, Qilianshan branch) — 30 min instead of 2 hours.
China Construction Bank — Jinqiu Road 中国建设银行锦秋路支行→ go there
Jinqiu Xintiandi, 809 Jinqiu Rd · 10 min walk
The closest large bank to the Jinqiu/Nanchen area. Opens accounts for foreign students, but a small branch might hesitate on an X2 — success varies depending on the teller. Their ATMs accept foreign Visa/Mastercard.
ICBC — Jufengyuan Road 中国工商银行聚丰园路支行→ go there
458 Jufengyuan Rd · 10–15 min walk from campus
Very close to the west gate. Excellent alternative if Bank of China is too busy.
Agricultural Bank of China — Jinqiu Road 中国农业银行（锦秋路716号）to verify→ go there
716 Jinqiu Rd · walking distance from the northwest of campus
Small neighborhood branch, also walking distance — but small branches sometimes send foreigners to a larger main branch.

Documents to bring — all of them, every time

  • Passport + visa page (and a photocopy of each)
  • SHU Admission Letter
  • Residence Registration Form 临时住宿登记单 (must be requested at your hotel reception; if you are in an apartment, you must register online here)
  • Active Chinese phone number — mandatory, everything is verified via SMS
  • ~100 RMB to activate the card (issuance fee 0–20 RMB)

At the counter, if asked why : 校园卡充值 (top up campus card), reimbursements, and daily expenses. Keep it simple, study-related.

If the bank refuses your X2 visa : Plan B described in the AFEC guide (foreign card linked to Alipay/WeChat, 0% fees up to 200 RMB per transaction) covers all daily life in Baoshan. Its real limit: impossible to receive money — hongbao, dorm deposit refund, transfer from a friend. This is the main reason to insist on the Chinese account; if you get it, link it immediately to Alipay and WeChat.
End of semester : empty or close the account before going home (passport + card at any branch of the bank) — a dormant account is a hassle to manage from abroad.
